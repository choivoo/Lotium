# KAGETSU (影月, "shadow moon") — Brief & Design Lock
Androgynous, boyish, slender (~6.8 heads), looks 15–18. Quiet, playful, mysterious; a living-shadow fighter.
The Marshadow *feel* is strong (charcoal palette, hood, hidden eyes, spectral green, ghost/fighting duality),
but the face, hair, clothing and signature elements are all original.

## Concept A/B/C → final
- A (soft/friendly: smile, blush, hood down) · B (core: halo shown) · C (spectral/combat: hood up, shadow hands)
- Rendered from the same rig: `masters/MSH_HUMAN_CONCEPT_[A|B|C]_v001.png`
- **Final = B**, plus A's soft default mouth and C's spectral kit, which is kept on toggles.
- **Signature chosen: broken-crescent spectral halo** (it gives the name 影月). The living shadow is the second device:
  pool → rear mimic shadow → two shadow arms → wisps and fragments.
