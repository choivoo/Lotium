# Mini Marshadow — Brief & Design Lock
A small spectral-shadow mascot. It stays quiet and cute in NORMAL and switches to SHADOW FIGHTING with one hotkey
(same model; only toggles and opacity change). References: `../reference/user_ref_*.jpg`.

**Locked (image: `masters/MSM_MINI_NORMAL_MASTER_v001.png`)**
- Head: round charcoal bulb (#5c5f69→#41444c), two swirl lobes with flick tips, curled smoke crest
- Face: dark mask band (#27282f); almond black sockets; red-orange iris (#e2522f) with gold core (#f7c94c); small frown
- Body: misty gray scarf ring with trailing end, black chest, gray lower body, stubby legs, mitten hands, thin wavy shadow tail
- FIGHT FX (spectral green #3cf0a0 / #8dffd4 / turquoise #27d9c4): green iris swap, crest and lobe flames, jagged eye marks,
  silhouette outline glow, shadow echo (mimicry), shadow hands and arm energy, particles, larger shadow pool, tail flame, broken ring
